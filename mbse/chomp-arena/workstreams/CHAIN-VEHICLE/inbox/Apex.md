# Apex chassis

Tier-four chassis with the broadest jaw, visible teeth, reinforced cheeks and a
three-piece crown profile. A dark body with light mouth edges creates maximum
value contrast for the world-locked camera.

## Unusual

- Built from 21 basic parts and 20 welds with no textures or scripted content.
- Crown rails and nose spine are non-colliding silhouette pieces, not equipment
  ports or gameplay modules.
- TriangleCount is declared as 276 from the authored primitive geometry.

## Decisions not covered by the contract

- Apex escalation uses vertical crown geometry while remaining below the
  six-stud height budget and preserving a dominant mouth silhouette.
- Cyan is confined to the single TeamColour brow band; permanent body accents
  are neutral so runtime team recolouring remains unambiguous.
