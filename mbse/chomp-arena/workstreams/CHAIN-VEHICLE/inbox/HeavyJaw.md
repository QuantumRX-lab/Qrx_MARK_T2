# HeavyJaw chassis

Tier-two chassis that broadens the Standard into a deliberate brawler. Orange
upper and lower jaws are framed by dark cheek guards, making the mouth direction
readable from the world-locked camera even without colour.

## Unusual

- Built from 13 basic parts and 12 welds with no textures or scripted content.
- Reinforced jaw guards are visual only; the transparent Chassis remains the
  sole colliding part.
- TriangleCount is declared as 168 from the authored primitive geometry.

## Decisions not covered by the contract

- Tier escalation is expressed through cheek width and jaw framing rather than
  increasing the collision envelope beyond the corridor budget.
- The canopy and single team-colour brow band remain in the same family position
  as Standard so chassis changes do not obscure player identity.
